Privacy Policy

Merglings · Effective 18 November 2026

Short version: Merglings does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Merglings has no account or sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

Game progress

Your progress (levels, creatures, settings, and the name you give your profile) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Age check

When the game starts for the first time it asks for a birth year. This is used only on your device to turn on Kids Mode, and it never leaves your device.

No ads, no purchases, no tracking

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • No chat or contact with other players.
  • The game does not connect to the internet to play.

Children

Merglings is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not have any. The app stores (Apple App Store and Google Play) may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request.
