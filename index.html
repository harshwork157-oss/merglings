How to play

  • Merge: drag a Mergling onto a matching one to make the next tier. Merge five at once for a bonus friend.
  • Goal: each level shows its goal at the top. Reach it before your moves run out.
  • Eggs and Island: trade Dew for eggs, hatch new friends, and they make Dew on your Island while you are away.

Your progress

Your game is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There is no account and nothing to restore.

Money and ads

Merglings has no in-app purchases and no ads. Everything in the game is earned by playing.
